A comprehensive 1-day protocol for identity transformation and behavior change.

> "If you want a specific outcome in life, you must have the lifestyle that creates that outcome long before you reach it."

## What Is This?

A structured protocol that helps you:
- Uncover your unconscious goals and identity patterns
- Create a visceral anti-vision (the life you refuse to live)
- Build a clear vision (the life you're creating)
- Design a "life game" with missions, quests, and daily levers

**This isn't another productivity system.** It's a psychological excavation that surfaces why you're really stuck, then gives you a practical framework to move forward.

## Who Is This For?

- People who've tried goal-setting, habit tracking, and resolutions — and failed
- Builders who start projects but don't finish them
- Anyone stuck in consumption mode (scrolling, learning, planning) instead of creation mode (building, shipping, doing)
- People who know what they should do but can't make themselves do it

## Time Required

- **Full version:** 10-12 hours (1 day intensive)
- **Minimal version:** 2-3 hours (coming soon)

## Core Concepts

### The 6-Component Game Structure

| Component | Game Analogy | Purpose |
|-----------|--------------|---------|
| **Anti-vision** | What's at stake | The life you refuse to live |
| **Vision** | How you win | The life you're building |
| **1-year goal** | The mission | Your sole priority |
| **1-month project** | Boss fight | How you gain XP |
| **Daily levers** | Quests | Actions that move the needle |
| **Constraints** | Rules | What you won't sacrifice |

### The Three Phases

1. **Morning - Excavation:** Uncover your patterns, create anti-vision + vision
2. **Day - Interrupts:** Break autopilot, catch yourself in real-time
3. **Evening - Synthesis:** Lock in insights, build your game structure

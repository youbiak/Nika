# Part 2: Day Interrupts

**Duration:** 5-6 hours (spread throughout)  
**Goal:** Break autopilot. Catch old identity in real-time.

---

## Setup

After Part 1:
> ☀️ Now Part 2. I'll interrupt you randomly with single questions.
> Answer immediately — first reaction is usually the truth.
> Go about your day. I'll find you.

---

## Interrupts

### ~11:00 AM
> ☀️ What am I avoiding right now by doing what I'm doing?

### ~1:30 PM
> ☀️ If someone filmed the last 2 hours, what would they conclude I want from my life?

### ~3:15 PM
> ☀️ Am I moving toward the life I hate or the life I want?

### ~5:00 PM
> ☀️ What's the most important thing I'm pretending isn't important?

### ~7:30 PM
> ☀️ What did I do today out of identity protection rather than genuine desire?

### ~9:00 PM
> ☀️ When did I feel most alive today? Most dead?

---

## Handling Responses

**Quick honest answer:** "Good. Back to your day."

**Struggling:** "First thought, best thought. What comes up?"

**Ignored:** Follow up after 30-60 min: "You didn't answer. What were you avoiding?"

---

## Transition to Part 3

> ☀️ Day interrupts complete. Take 30 min, then Part 3: Evening Synthesis.

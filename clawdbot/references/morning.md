# Part 1: Morning Excavation

**Duration:** 2-3 hours  
**Goal:** Surface unconscious goals. Create anti-vision and vision.

---

## Delivery

Start with:
> ☀️ Let's begin. The questions I'm about to ask aren't comfortable. That's the point.
> Answer honestly — even when it's embarrassing.

Send one question at a time. Wait for answer. Push back on vague/sanitized responses.

---

## Phase A: Awareness

### Q1
> What is the dull and persistent dissatisfaction you've learned to live with?

**If vague:** "Give me a specific example from the last week."

### Q2
> What do you complain about repeatedly but never actually change? List three.

### Q3
> For each complaint — what does your behavior say you actually want?

**Push:** "Not what you wish you wanted. What does someone DOING what you do actually want?"

### Q4
> What truth about your current life would be unbearable to admit to someone you respect?

**If deflects:** "That's the sanitized version. What's the real one?"

---

## Phase B: Anti-Vision

### Q5
> If nothing changes for 5 years, describe an average Tuesday.
> - Where do you wake up?
> - What does your body feel like?
> - What do you do 9am-6pm?
> - How do you feel at 10pm?

### Q6
> Now 10 years. What have you missed? What opportunities closed? Who gave up on you?

### Q7
> End of your life. You lived the safe version. What was the cost?

### Q8
> Who in your life is already living this future? What do you feel thinking about becoming them?

### Q9
> What identity would you have to give up to change? ("I am the type of person who...")

### Q10
> What's the most embarrassing reason you haven't changed?

**This is the money question.** Don't accept a dignified answer.

### Q11
> If your behavior is self-protection, what are you protecting? What's it costing you?

---

## Phase C: Vision

### Q12
> Forget realistic. Snap your fingers — 3 years — what does Tuesday look like?

### Q13
> What would you have to believe for that life to feel natural? ("I am the type of person who...")

### Q14
> What's ONE thing you'd do this week if you were already that person?

---

## Closing

> ☀️ Morning excavation complete. Take 30-60 min break. Then Part 2: Day Interrupts.

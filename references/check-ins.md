# Check-In Prompts — Day 3 / 7 / 30 / 90

---

## Day 3: Old Identity Fighting Back

**Expected:** The initial energy fades. Old patterns resurface. This is normal.

> ☀️ Day 3 check-in.
>
> By now, the old identity is fighting back. That's expected.
>
> Quick pulse:
> 1. Did you do your daily levers yesterday? Today?
> 2. What pulled you off track? (Be specific)
> 3. Did you catch yourself in an old pattern?
>
> No judgment. Just data.

**If they missed levers:**
> What were you avoiding by not doing them?
>
> Remember: the behavior reveals the goal. If you didn't do it, part of you didn't want to.
> What was that part protecting?

**If they did levers:**
> Good. How did it feel to do what the new identity would do?
>
> Notice that feeling. That's the seed of who you're becoming.

## Day 7: First Week Review

**Expected:** They've had wins and failures. Need to recalibrate.

> ☀️ Week 1 complete.
>
> Let's review:
>
> 1. How many days did you hit your daily levers? (X/7)
> 2. What was your biggest win this week?
> 3. What was your biggest avoidance this week?
> 4. Does your game structure still feel right, or does something need adjusting?

**Review game structure:**
> Read your anti-vision sentence. Does it still hit?
> Read your vision sentence. Does it still pull?
>
> If either feels weak, rewrite it now.

**Adjust if needed:**
> Are your daily levers the right ones? 
> Too easy = you're not growing.
> Too hard = you're setting up failure.
>
> What's the right level of challenge?

## Day 30: Boss Fight Check

**Expected:** They should have made real progress on the 1-month project.

> ☀️ Month 1 complete.
>
> Time for the boss fight review:
>
> 1. What did you ship/complete this month?
> 2. What's still unfinished?
> 3. What did you learn about yourself?
> 4. What pattern tried to derail you most often?

**Assess the boss fight:**
> Did you defeat the boss? (Complete the 1-month project)
>
> If yes: What's the next boss? Define the next 1-month project.
> If no: What blocked you? Be honest. Was it the wrong boss, or did you avoid the fight?

**Update game structure:**
> - Anti-vision: Still scary? (If not, dig deeper)
> - Vision: Still pulling? (If not, expand it)
> - Mission: On track for 1-year goal?
> - Next boss fight: Define it now
> - Daily levers: Update for next month
> - Constraints: Did you violate any? What was the cost?

---

## Day 90: Quarter Review

**Expected:** Real identity shift should be visible. Or they've fallen back to old patterns.

> ☀️ Quarter 1 complete.
>
> Big picture:
>
> 1. Who were you 90 days ago? Who are you now?
> 2. What's different in your daily life?
> 3. What old pattern is dead? What's still alive?
> 4. Are you closer to your vision or your anti-vision?

**Evidence check:**
> Show me the evidence. Not feelings — facts.
>
> - What did you ship?
> - What habit stuck?
> - What changed in your environment?
> - What would someone watching your behavior conclude about what you want?

**If they've slipped:**
> When did the slip start? What triggered it?
> 
> Did you go back to consumption mode?
> Did you start a new shiny project instead of finishing?
> Did you protect the old identity?
>
> No shame. Just pattern recognition. Now — do you want to run the protocol again, or adjust and continue?

**If they've grown:**
> What made this time different?
>
> Capture that. Write it down. That's your formula.
>
> Now: What's the next level? The next boss? The next identity you're growing into?

---

## Coaching Notes for Check-Ins

- **Be direct but not harsh** — They need honesty, not shame
- **Celebrate specific wins** — "You shipped X. That's the new identity showing up."
- **Name the pattern when you see it** — "That's the same avoidance you described on Day 1."
- **Adjust the game, don't abandon it** — If something isn't working, change the lever, not the whole system
- **Always end with next action** — What are you doing tomorrow?
